## 3 · Rulings the design needs that nobody has made

| # | question | recommended default |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Is there a customer tier? `Platinum` is drawn as a chip beside `All · Active · Banned` with no column behind it. | No tier. Delete the chip. If one is wanted later it is a lookup table, not a constant, and it belongs beside the review aggregate rather than ahead of it. |
| Q2 | Which `role` does the drawn `Role` field mean — the permission level on `auth.users.role` or the job label on `auth.employees.role`? And is `Owner` a permission level? | Two separate fields. `auth.users.role` stays the permission level and gains a CHECK (`user`, `verified_user`, `admin`); `auth.employees.role` becomes `role_id` into a lookup of job titles (`Owner`, `Admin`, `Agent`, `Driver`). `Owner` is a job title, not a fourth level — one admin level is enough until a permission actually differs. |
| Q3 | What are a **user's** states? Customers draws `Active · Banned`, Users draws `Active · Invited · Disabled`, over one table. | `Active · Banned · Deletion requested`, from `banned` and `deletion_requested_at`. Drop `Invited` and `Disabled` from the Users list — an invite belongs to employees, and "disabled" is what `banned` already means. |
| Q4 | Is a **conversation** assignable, separately from the person it is with? | No. The owner is the person's `assigned_to_id`, and the Inbox header says `Owner` rather than `Assigned to`. A second ownership axis doubles the reassignment problem for one screen. |
| Q5 | Does assignment keep **history** — who owned this lead before, and when did it move? | Yes, and it comes free: once the activity table of §1 row 8 exists, a reassignment is one of its verbs. No separate assignment-history table. |
| Q6 | Does **ban** keep history, and does a temporary ban show its expiry? | Same answer: ban and unban are activity verbs, with the reason carried on the row. `ban_expires` shows on the badge (`Banned · until Nov 3`), because a ban that quietly lapses is worse than one that never happened. |
| Q7 | Do `contacted` / `responded` / `converted` become timestamps? | Yes. Facts are timestamps (ruling), `lead_stage` already derives in SQL, and three of the drawn metrics and the whole activity feed need the dates. Convert in one pass with the stage derivation. |
| Q8 | Is `payments.ledger.type` an enum, a lookup table, or free text — and does it gain a `reason`? | A Postgres enum (`Credit`, `Debit`, `Reserve`, `Released`); the set is closed, code branches on it, and nobody administers it. Plus `reason text` and the two actor columns. The label an operator reads derives in SQL from `type` + the joined order. |
| Q9 | Does the Credit card show `Reserve` and `Released` rows? | Yes, both, and the header splits into `Available` / `Held`. Hiding them makes `dorado_funds` look wrong to the one person who can act on it. |
| Q10 | Does a payout account have a default? | Yes — one per customer, enforced by a partial unique index, like the default shipping address. The badge is already drawn and a payout screen has to pick something. |
| Q11 | Is SMS consent kept as **history**, or only as current state? | History. The current-state columns stay as the read; an append-only `crm.consent_events` is the record. A carrier dispute asks *when did they opt out*, and today's schema overwrites the only answer. |
| Q12 | Can one person be both an employee and a customer, and do `assigned_to_id` (→ `auth.users`) and `crm.calls.employee_id` (→ `auth.employees`) stay two identities? | One `auth.users` row per human, and **one** assignment identity: point every `assigned_to_id` at `auth.employees(id)`, the way `crm.calls` and the three `fulfillments.*` tables already do. Internal messaging does not go through `crm.sms_messages`. |
| Q13 | Who prices a lead estimate? | The pricing domain. `api/src/domains/pricing/index.ts` exports six functions and none of them prices an estimate, so the in-flight lane needs a seventh — `priceEstimate` — rather than arithmetic in `crm`. Only pricing prices (ruling), and `lint:pricing-owner` will say so. |
| Q14 | Does the activity feed come from a new append-only table, or from per-field timestamps on each subject? | A table. Four of the six drawn verbs are field-level changes that no `updated_by_id` can reconstruct, and a feed that spans leads, users, credit and orders cannot be a `UNION` over columns that do not exist. |
| Q15 | Is `Notes` one table shared by customers, leads and employees, or one per entity? | One `crm.notes` with two nullable subject ids (`user_id`, `lead_id`) and a CHECK that exactly one is set — so a lead's notes survive conversion by re-pointing, which is what the Convert dialog already promises. Employees get notes only if asked for. |
| Q16 | Does `Last sign-in` become a stamped column on `auth.users`? | Yes. Deriving it from `auth.sessions` reads as "last sign-in we have not yet pruned", which is a different and less useful fact. |
